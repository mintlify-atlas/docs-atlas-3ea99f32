Better Lyrics is a free, open-source browser extension that brings studio-quality, time-synced lyrics to YouTube Music. With syllable-level highlighting, real-time translations, a CSS theme marketplace, and the Unison community lyrics platform, it transforms how you experience music in the browser.

## What is Better Lyrics?

Better Lyrics injects a fully interactive lyrics panel into YouTube Music. It fetches from 16 different providers — including its own syllable-synced catalog, Musixmatch, LRCLib, and the community-contributed Unison database — and picks the highest-quality match automatically. You can seek to any point in a song by clicking a lyric line, enable karaoke subtitles during music video playback, translate lyrics on the fly, or float lyrics in a picture-in-picture window while you work.

## Get started in seconds

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the extension">
    Download Better Lyrics from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/better-lyrics/effdbpeggelllpfkjppbokhmmiinhlmg), [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/better-lyrics/), or [Microsoft Edge Add-ons](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/better-lyrics-lyrics-for/mjfeaklppoegooljmjicjdbiccgjdlhd).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open YouTube Music">
    Navigate to [music.youtube.com](https://music.youtube.com) and start playing any song.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Lyrics tab">
    The lyrics panel loads automatically. Click any line to seek to that moment in the song.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize to your taste">
    Click the Better Lyrics icon in your extensions toolbar to adjust providers, enable translations, or explore themes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Better Lyrics works exclusively on [music.youtube.com](https://music.youtube.com) — it does not run on youtube.com.
</Note>
